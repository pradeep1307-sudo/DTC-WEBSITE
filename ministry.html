Children & Families

KIDS MINISTRY

Helping Children Grow in Faith

Audience: Children attending Denver Tamil Church and visiting families.

Schedule: Every Sunday at 5:00 PM, during the worship service.

Denver Tamil Church Kids Ministry provides a joyful, safe, and Bible-centered environment where children can learn about Jesus and grow in faith. Children participate in age-appropriate Bible lessons, prayer, worship, activities, and fellowship while the main worship service is in progress.

Programs & Activities

  • Kids Ministry every Sunday at 5:00 PM during the worship service.
  • Vacation Bible School (VBS) held every year.
  • Approximately six children’s presentations each year during church services.
  • Presentations may include Bible stories, Scripture recitation, songs, skits, and creative activities.
  • Opportunities to develop confidence, friendships, biblical knowledge, and a heart for worship and service.

Next Generation

TEEN & YOUNG ADULT

Faith, Friendship & Leadership

Audience: Teenagers and young adults.

Schedule: Contact us for upcoming gatherings and events.

The Teen & Young Adult Ministry helps the next generation build a personal relationship with Jesus, grow through God’s Word, and form strong Christian friendships. It provides a welcoming place where teens and young adults can ask questions, discover their gifts, worship together, and become confident Christian leaders.

Programs & Activities

  • Bible-based teaching and group discussions.
  • Worship, prayer, and fellowship gatherings.
  • Opportunities to participate in and lead church worship services.
  • Leadership development, mentoring, and discipleship.
  • Community service, outreach, and church volunteer opportunities.
  • Social activities that encourage healthy Christian friendships.

Faithful Leadership

MEN’S MINISTRY

Growing as Servant Leaders

Audience: Men of all ages and life stages.

Schedule: Contact us for upcoming gatherings and events.

Denver Tamil Church Men’s Ministry encourages men to grow as faithful followers of Christ, servant leaders, husbands, fathers, mentors, and friends. Through prayer, fellowship, and the study of God’s Word, men support one another and grow stronger in their faith and daily lives.

Programs & Activities

  • Prayer and Bible study gatherings.
  • Christian fellowship and encouragement.
  • Mentoring and spiritual growth.
  • Service opportunities within the church and community.
  • Special events and family-focused activities.

WOMEN’S MINISTRY

Growing Together in Christ

Audience: Women of all ages and life stages.

Schedule: Contact us for upcoming gatherings and events.

Denver Tamil Church Women’s Ministry is a caring community where women can grow in Christ, pray together, study God’s Word, encourage one another, and build lasting friendships. The ministry equips women to use their gifts to bless their families, church, and community.

Programs & Activities

  • Prayer and Bible study gatherings.
  • Fellowship, encouragement, and mutual support.

  • Community outreach and service opportunities.
  • Special gatherings and seasonal events.
